# Chapter 8 Lab: Incident Response: Attack Frameworks and Core IR Activities

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Given a described active ransomware incident, sequence the correct IR actions: isolate affected hosts (containment), remove malicious artifacts and reset credentials (eradication), restore from clean backups (recovery).

## Key takeaway

IR phases have a strict logical order — containment before eradication before recovery — and the exam tests whether you know that sequence cold.
