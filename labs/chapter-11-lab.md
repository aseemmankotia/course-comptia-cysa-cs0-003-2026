# Chapter 11 Lab: Reporting and Communication: Incident Response Reporting and Escalation

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Given a scenario with a suspected but unconfirmed breach involving customer data, decide whether to formally declare an incident, and identify the correct escalation path and stakeholders who must be notified and when.

## Key takeaway

Incident escalation timing and stakeholder selection are often as important on the exam as the technical response itself.
