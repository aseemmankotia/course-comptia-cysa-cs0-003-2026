# Chapter 4 Lab: Security Operations: Automation, Orchestration, and SOAR for Efficiency

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Design a simple SOAR playbook flowchart for 'phishing email reported by user': auto-extract URLs/attachments, check reputation, auto-quarantine if malicious, notify analyst — identify which steps are safe to fully automate versus which require human approval.

## Key takeaway

SOAR reduces repetitive analyst work through orchestration and automation, but high-impact response actions still require human-in-the-loop approval.
