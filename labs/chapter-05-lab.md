# Chapter 5 Lab: Vulnerability Management: Scanning Methods and Concepts

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Given a scenario describing a legacy SCADA device that crashes under active scans, decide the correct scan configuration (passive monitoring, non-intrusive check, or documented exclusion with compensating monitoring).

## Key takeaway

The right scan method depends on system criticality and fragility, not just on achieving the most accurate results.
