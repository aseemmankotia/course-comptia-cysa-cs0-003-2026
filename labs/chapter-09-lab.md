# Chapter 9 Lab: Incident Response: Preparation and Post-Incident Activity

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Given a completed incident timeline, draft the key sections of a lessons-learned report: what happened, root cause, what worked, what didn't, and specific follow-up actions with owners and deadlines.

## Key takeaway

Incident response isn't finished at recovery — preparation and post-incident review are what prevent the same incident from recurring.
