# Chapter 7 Lab: Vulnerability Management: Mitigating Controls and Vulnerability Response

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Given a critical vulnerability in a vendor appliance with no available patch for 30 days, select the correct compensating control to reduce risk until remediation is possible.

## Key takeaway

When remediation isn't immediately possible, a documented compensating control — not silence or risk denial — is the correct exam answer.
