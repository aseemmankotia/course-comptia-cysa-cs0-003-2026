# Chapter 12 Lab: Full Exam Simulation and Time-Management Strategy

**Estimated time:** 165 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a timed 85-question style practice exam simulation, allocating roughly 90 seconds per multiple-choice question and reserving a dedicated block for performance-based questions; review every missed question against its exam domain.

## Key takeaway

Time management — not just knowledge — determines whether you finish all 85 questions with a fair chance at each one; budget PBQ time deliberately.
